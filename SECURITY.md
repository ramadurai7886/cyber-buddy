# Security & Privacy Policy

Cyber Buddy is committed to protecting the privacy, psychological safety, and legal rights of women cybercrime victims.

## Privacy Architecture (Zero-Knowledge Design)

- **Client-Side Storage**: In Emergency Mode, all data (descriptions, uploaded screenshots, voice input, PIN codes) is stored purely in the user's browser `localStorage` and memory. No data is transmitted to or retained on any external server.
- **Automated Evidence Obfuscation**: Evidence previews are automatically blurred on the client device to prevent retraumatization or accidental exposure.
- **Anti-Surveillance Wipe**: The "Wipe Traces & Exit" feature provides a 1-click total purge of all local storage keys, session cache, and case histories.
- **Discreet Camouflage Mode**: The Quick Exit (`Esc` / `Alt+Q`) immediately replaces the active interface with a benign, fully working Calculator or Weather Radar application.

## Reporting a Security Vulnerability

If you discover a vulnerability or potential data exposure risk in Cyber Buddy:

1. **Do not open a public GitHub issue.**
2. Please email the maintainers or report responsibly via [GitHub Security Advisories](https://github.com/ramadurai7886/cyber-buddy/security/advisories).
3. Provide reproduction steps, affected browsers/environments, and impact assessment.
4. We aim to acknowledge security reports within 24 hours.
